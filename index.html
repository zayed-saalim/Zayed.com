Saalim Zayed is an entrepreneur, author, and business leader dedicated to building brands, creating meaningful ideas, and inspiring purposeful living.

As the CEO of Hukm Qalbi, he leads an initiative dedicated to publishing thought-provoking works on purpose, discipline, and personal growth. He is also the Founder, CEO, and Manager of a U.S.-based modern pet accessories brand, where he oversees brand strategy, operations, and business development.

Through writing and entrepreneurship, Saalim aims to create work that leaves a lasting impact — building businesses that solve real problems and books that challenge readers to think deeply about life, purpose, and the future.
